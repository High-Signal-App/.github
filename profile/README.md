# High Signal

**Core product organization** — High Signal is the product; the other repositories provide relevant research and data capabilities around it.

Evidence-first research and data products for finding what is worth knowing.

- **[High Signal](https://github.com/High-Signal-App/high-signal)** — a versioned signal log for AI infrastructure and semiconductors · [highsignal.app](https://highsignal.app)
- **[Research Papers](https://github.com/High-Signal-App/research-papers)** — supporting research data: a 488k-paper platform with semantic search, citation graphs, and peer-review signals
- **[EverythingRated](https://github.com/High-Signal-App/everythingrated)** — supporting structured ratings data for adoption and comparison decisions
- **[drank](https://github.com/High-Signal-App/drank)** — supporting domain-rating research
